# Model License Requirements (Draft)

This is a requirements document, not an active license.

The final paper-weight license should be approved by the model owner and legal
counsel and should, at minimum:

- identify the licensor and the exact covered checkpoint;
- permit use only for non-commercial research;
- define "model", "derivative model", and "commercial use";
- state attribution and paper-citation requirements;
- prohibit redistribution unless explicitly approved;
- address warranty, liability, termination, and governing law;
- explain how commercial-use requests are handled;
- preserve applicable third-party rights and restrictions.

Hugging Face gating is an access-control mechanism, not a substitute for these
license terms. The final model card must link to the approved license, and the
access form must require affirmative acceptance.
